# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in agent-box to be safe, welcoming, and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy, especially in security reports and diagnostic output.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated. Publishing another person's private information or credentials is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community or project.

Report a concern through a private contact method published on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/abraxarion). If no private maintainer channel is available, or the concern involves the maintainer, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For security vulnerabilities, use a private [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/abraxarion/agent-box/security/advisories/new).

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and promptly as the available project channels allow.
